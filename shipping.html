Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Digital Delivery

CRAFTCON '26 is an in-person event, but the ticketing and registration process is entirely digital. Upon successful registration, your digital inventory pass will be generated instantly on the screen and sent to your registered email address.

2. Physical Shipping

There are no physical tickets, merchandise, or products shipped to participants prior to the event. Any event-related swags, ID badges, or hardware kits will be distributed on-site during the check-in process at Desh Bhagat University.

3. Delivery Timeline

Digital tickets are delivered immediately upon registration confirmation. If you do not receive an email confirmation within 24 hours, please contact our support team.
