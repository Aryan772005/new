Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Hackathon Registration

CRAFTCON '26 main hackathon registration and participation are 100% free of charge. Therefore, there are no refund procedures for the main event.

2. Gaming Arena Entry Fees

Registration fees paid for the CRAFTCON '26 Gaming Arena (e.g., BGMI, Ludo) are strictly non-refundable. Once a team or individual registers for a gaming tournament, the slot is confirmed and the fee cannot be refunded under any circumstances, including no-shows or disqualifications.

3. Event Cancellation

In the highly unlikely event that a Gaming Arena tournament is completely canceled by the organizers, participants will be eligible for a full refund of their gaming registration fee. Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

4. Contact Us

For any queries regarding your registration, please email us at aryansinghtariani@gmail.com.
